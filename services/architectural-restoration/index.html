Restoration, not redecoration

Breathing new life into heritage buildings and rare spaces, we approach restoration with humility, technical expertise and reverence for the past, working closely with conservation architects.

Since 2012

What this involves

  • Structural intervention first — the bones before the finishes
  • Original detail surveyed, catalogued and protected, not replaced
  • Conservation architects engaged from the start
  • Modern livability introduced without erasing the building

How we work

Final measurements are taken jointly at site with a representative you nominate and agreed by both parties before any invoice is generated. Any change in scope — up or down — is reflected in the final invoice.
